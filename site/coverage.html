Each constitution scored, with the passages behind every figure

AI companies publish constitutions: documents setting out how their models should* behave. This board scores each one twice, out of 10 each, and leads with the two added together. Every figure opens on what is behind it, and on the passages in the document itself.

The document as a whole. Whether the constitution settles a clash between two of its own rules, whether a reader can tell which rules are absolute and which can be lifted and by whom, whether the rules come with reasons, whether they reach the situations the models are used in, and whether the document contradicts itself somewhere without saying which rule wins.

The behaviours. How far the constitution goes on each behaviour the index asks about, from saying nothing about it to setting rules and showing them applied to worked examples.

Missing a constitution, or a behaviour the index should be asking about? Propose one.
