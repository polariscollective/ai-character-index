## Two things behind the numbers

- **The rule drops unanimous "related".** Retention needs a sum of 4, so every retained passage has at least one judge at core. A passage all three judges call related sums to 3 and is never shown. On this document that is 36 unanimous-related passage-cells, and 170 at a sum of 3. Deepseek gave 0 on 53 of the 58 passages I would add. Adding by hand at related repairs exactly what the rule loses.
- **Self-preference.** On secret loyalties and objectivity the audit's figure equals fable's, and on no sycophancy fable is the high outlier. Those are the three upward moves I would not make without a human reader, whatever their direction.
